Terms of use

The rules for using this platform.

ResQNet is an academic project. It is not an official relief system, it awards no actual entitlement, and it is not connected to emergency services or to any banking system.

Emergencies

Do not rely on ResQNet in a life-threatening emergency. Submitting a report does not summon rescue, medical or fire services. Call your national emergency number instead. ResQNet records damage for relief assessment after the fact.

Accurate reporting

Report damage truthfully. Every report is verified on site by a field officer, and the compensation calculation is based on that verification rather than on the original claim. Deliberately false claims for public relief funds are a serious matter in any real deployment of a system like this.

Your account

  • Keep your password confidential and do not share your account.
  • Register only on your own behalf.
  • Field officer and administrator accounts are issued by an administrator and must be used only for official duties.

Photographs you upload

Upload only photographs of the damage you are reporting, and only images you took or have the right to use. Uploads are validated and limited in size and type.

Relief decisions

Calculated compensation is an assessment produced by the relief matrix, not a guarantee of payment. An administrator reviews every case and may approve, adjust, reject, or request a re-inspection — always with a recorded written reason.

Payouts

Payout tracking in this deployment is simulated. Statuses move through pending, initiated and completed for demonstration purposes. No funds are transferred.

Availability

The platform is provided as-is for academic demonstration, with no guarantee of availability, accuracy or fitness for operational use.
