How ResQNet works

Five stages, and a case can only ever move forward through them in order. An administrator cannot approve relief for a property nobody has inspected, and a payout cannot complete before it has been approved.

Stage 1

Report

A citizen submits damage details, photos and a location — online or offline.

Stage 2

Prioritise

A rule-based engine scores the report and classifies it Low to Critical.

Stage 3

Verify

The dispatched officer inspects the property and signs off on the findings.

Stage 4

Approve

An administrator reviews the evidence and the itemised relief calculation.

Stage 5

Relief

The approved payout is tracked to completion and the citizen is kept informed.

Built for the conditions relief work actually happens in

Bad networks, stressed users, and decisions that have to be defensible months later.

⛅

Offline reporting

Reports are saved on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns — without ever creating a duplicate.

◎

GPS-based reporting

Location from browser GPS, manual entry, or photo metadata — validated server-side, never blindly trusted.

⚖

Smart triage

Urgency is a sum of published rules. Every score can be broken down line by line.

➤

Automatic officer assignment

Matched on availability, zone, straight-line distance and current workload — with the reasoning recorded.

✔

Field verification

Relief follows what a trained officer confirmed on site, not what was claimed.

₹

Compensation calculation

A configurable relief matrix produces an itemised, reproducible breakdown.

◷

Relief tracking

Citizens follow their case from submission through inspection, approval and payout.

🗎

Audit transparency

Every significant action is appended to a permanent history the citizen can read.

◱

Low-bandwidth friendly

Photos are compressed in the browser before upload, and pages stay light.
